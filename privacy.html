Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Content Factory ("the app") is a private, self-hosted tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It is used only by its owner to publish videos to accounts the owner controls.

What data the app accesses

  • OAuth access tokens for the owner's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts, granted by the owner.
  • Basic account information (account name, follower count) and statistics for the owner's own videos (views, likes, comments, shares), used only to show the owner their performance.

How data is stored

All data and tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or sent to any server operated by the app.

Third-party platforms

When videos are published, they are sent directly to Google (YouTube), TikTok and Meta (Instagram) through their official APIs and are subject to those platforms' privacy policies.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect any account inside the app at any time, which deletes the stored token. Access can also be revoked from each platform's account settings.

Contact

For any question about this policy, contact the owner via the email listed on the connected accounts' profiles.
